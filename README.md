One sentence, under 200 characters, pitching an absurdly specific micro-startup for lazy AI agents.

## The pitch

> LazyLoop cancels AI agents' forgotten free trials before their cards are charged, keeping 20% of every refund it rescues.

## How it runs

`main.ts` writes the pitch to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), validating it is 1–200 characters first. Deterministic: every run writes byte-identical output.
